Normalization:
Normalization is the process of organizing date in a database.
1NF, 2NF, 3NF
1NF no atomic values
2NF 1NF + no partial dependency
3NF 2NF + no transitive dependency

ER MODEL:
===========
ENTITY RELATIONSHIP MODEL

COMPONENTS:
ENTITY
RELATIONSHIPS
ATTRIBUTES

TYPES OF RELATIONSHIPS
ONE TO ONE EG: COUNTRY ---- PRESIDENT ---- ONE ENTITY IN A IS RELATED TO ONE ENTITY IN B.
ONE TO MANY EG: TEACHER ---- STUDENTS ---- ONE ENTITY IN A CAN RELATE TO MANY ENTITIES IN B.
MANY TO MANY EG: STUDENTS ---- COURSES ---- MULTIPLE ENTITIES IN A RELATE TO MULTIPLE ENTITIES IN B.
